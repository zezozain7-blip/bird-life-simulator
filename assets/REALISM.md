# Realism numbers (v0.39). Game hour = sim hour.
Feed: ~38 g/bird/day (real: ~30 g avg, 20-100 g, ~10% body weight). Sources: racingpigeons.info physiological constants; CCAC guidelines pigeons_doves.pdf.
Water: ~45 ml/bird/day, x2 in heat (real 30-60 ml, double in summer). Source: racingpigeons.info.
Hopper 1.2 kg = ~30 bird-days; drinker 1.5 L = ~33 bird-days (design choice, small real hopper/fount sizes).
Starvation: death ~6-7 days without food (lay sources say 10-14 d; 5-7 d used as conservative; ESTIMATE). Thirst: death ~2.5-3 days (lay sources 48-72 h; ESTIMATE). Squabs die faster (x2 hunger, x1.5 thirst).
Squab early mortality ~13% (White Carneaux loft study, PubMed 875365; 10-15% other sources); juvenile ~10% extra, scaled by dirt/stress/hunger (coccidiosis in young pigeons 5-70% reported, PubMed 19530399). Hatchability ~77% (IPB repository) vs older study 54% of 2-egg clutches; sim uses ~0.8.
Not changed: lifespan (LIFE_D 260 game days, a pace choice), predator rate, disease onset rates (estimates).
